# Cooperative puzzle game

A two-player cooperative browser puzzle game set aboard a sinking steamship, in which each Player is sealed on a separate Deck holding the Clues for the other's Puzzles, and talking out loud is the only way to the Lifeboat.

## People

**Person**:
Someone the game recognises across Voyages, either a Guest or an Account, who plays as a Player on one Deck per Voyage; the same two Persons are always the same Team, whichever Decks they take.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, profile

**Guest**:
A Person recognised only by the browser they first played in, until they become an Account without losing their history.
_Avoid_: Anonymous, temp user

**Account**:
A Person who signs in with credentials they chose and is recognised in any browser.
_Avoid_: Login, profile, member

**Player**:
One of the two people in a Team, each on their own Deck and known only by it, never by a character name.
_Avoid_: User, client, participant, character, Mate, Stoker

**Team**:
Exactly two Players sailing Voyages together.
_Avoid_: Crew, pair, party, group

**Player name**:
The name the person playing goes by outside the ship, shown beside their partner's on the Leaderboard. Never a character name.
_Avoid_: Username, handle, display name, nickname

## Ship

**Voyage**:
One Team's play of the game, from the first Puzzle to the Lifeboat, on a ship with its own drawn name.
_Avoid_: Attempt, playthrough, run, session, game, match, escape

**Deck**:
One of the two sealed-off sides of the ship in a Voyage, the Upper Deck or the Lower Deck, chosen by one Player and hidden from the other, holding the Clues for the other Deck's Puzzles.
_Avoid_: Room, side, half, level, station, view, screen

**Room**:
One space within a Deck, such as the Wheelhouse on the Upper Deck or the Engine Room on the Lower Deck.
_Avoid_: Compartment, area, scene, level

**Voice Pipe**:
The brass speaking tube joining the two Decks, the in-fiction reason Players can talk and the only channel between them.
_Avoid_: Chat, intercom, radio

**Lifeboat**:
Where every Voyage is heading: launching it with both Players aboard is the only way to win.
_Avoid_: Exit, goal, escape hatch

## Puzzles

**Puzzle Type**:
A kind of puzzle with its own shape of split information: lock-and-clue, blind navigator, or split instrument.
_Avoid_: Minigame, challenge, category

**Puzzle**:
One authored design of a Puzzle Type that yields fresh answers and Clues every time it is drawn, such as the mural keypad.
_Avoid_: Template, generator, level

**Clue**:
Something on one Deck that the other Player needs to solve a Puzzle on their own Deck.
_Avoid_: Hint, answer

**Signal**:
A narrow, deliberate effect that crosses from one Deck to the other as part of a Puzzle, such as a lamp that flickers on the other side. A puzzle mechanic, never a way to talk.
_Avoid_: Message, chat, ping, channel

**Draw**:
The set of Puzzles chosen for one Voyage, together with the answers and Clues generated for them.
_Avoid_: Deal, run, seed

## Competition

**Score**:
The result of one Team's attempt, computed by the game from what happened in it. It always belongs to the Team, never to one Player.
_Avoid_: Points, time, result

**Leaderboard**:
The lasting ranking of Teams by their Scores.
_Avoid_: High scores, rankings, scoreboard
