# Cooperative puzzle game

A two-player cooperative browser puzzle game in which each Player is shut in a separate Room and can only get out by talking to the other.

## Language

**Player**:
One of the two people in a Team, each in their own Room.
_Avoid_: User, client, side

**Team**:
Exactly two Players attempting the game together.
_Avoid_: Crew, pair, party, group

**Room**:
The space one Player is in, which holds the Clues for the other Player's puzzles and can't be seen by the other Player.
_Avoid_: Level, side, half, screen

**Clue**:
Something in one Room that the other Player needs to solve a puzzle in their own Room.
_Avoid_: Hint, answer

**Puzzle type**:
A reusable kind of puzzle that can appear inside the Rooms, such as lock-and-clue, blind navigator or split instrument.
_Avoid_: Minigame, challenge

**Signal**:
A narrow, deliberate effect that crosses from one Room to the other as part of a puzzle, such as a lamp that flickers on the other side. A puzzle mechanic, never a way to talk.
_Avoid_: Message, chat, ping, channel
