# Cooperative puzzle game

A two-player cooperative browser puzzle game in which each Player sits in a separate room holding the clues for the other's puzzles, and talking is the way through.

## People

**Player**:
One of the two people in a Team, in their own room.
_Avoid_: User, participant

**Team**:
Exactly two Players attempting the game together.
_Avoid_: Party, group, pair

**Room**:
One of the two separated spaces in an attempt, chosen by and held by one Player, holding the clues for the other Room's puzzles.
_Avoid_: Side, station, view

## Puzzles

**Puzzle Type**:
A kind of puzzle with its own shape of split information: lock-and-clue, blind navigator, or split instrument.
_Avoid_: Mechanic, category

**Puzzle**:
One authored design of a Puzzle Type that yields fresh answers and clues every time it is drawn, such as the mural keypad.
_Avoid_: Template, generator, level

**Draw**:
The set of Puzzles chosen for one attempt, together with the answers and clues generated for them.
_Avoid_: Deal, run, seed
